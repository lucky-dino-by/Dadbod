Club Dry Sauna Blood Pressure Protocol

Mon & Thu (15 Min)

Using the dry sauna (175°F–190°F) for 12 minutes after lifting triggers Nitric Oxide Vessel Relaxation (eNOS) ⓘ, which acts like a natural blood pressure reducer for 24 hours. Skip icy cold plunges (<55°F)—freezing water causes sudden blood vessel clamping ("cold shock") that sharply spikes blood pressure.

1. Mins 0–12: Dry Sauna

Sit on the middle bench right after your workout. Breathe slowly (4s in, 4s hold, 4s out) to calm your nervous system.

2. Mins 12–15: Mild Cool Shower

Take a lukewarm-to-cool shower (65°F–70°F) to stop sweating gently without shocking your arteries.

3. Low-Sodium Hydration

Drink 16 oz water with a potassium/magnesium electrolyte powder (avoid high-salt packets like LMNT).
